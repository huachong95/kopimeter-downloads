Before you start

What you'll need

  • ✓A Claude Pro or Max subscription. KopiMeter reads the session and weekly limits those plans come with.
  • ✓A Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+ computer with Bluetooth — the machine KopiMeter pairs with, usually the one you work on.
  • ✓Claude Code installed on it. You sign in with it once, in step 4, then never need to open it again.
  • ✓The USB-C cable in the box and a spare port or charger.

2✓

Install the KopiMeter app

Go to the download page (or scan the box QR), get the installer for your system, and run it. When it finishes, a small cup icon appears in your system tray, bottom-rightmenu bar, top-right.

Windows will warn you first. You'll see “Windows protected your PC” with an unknown publisher. That is expected: KopiMeter is a small independent project and the installer isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. If you'd rather check the download first, compare it against SHA256SUMS.txt on the download page.
macOS needs one extra click the first time. Open the .dmg, drag KopiMeter to Applications, then — this part matters — right-click the app and choose Open, and confirm. A plain double-click gives you “cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified” with no way past it. That is expected: KopiMeter is a small independent project and the app isn't notarised. You only do this once; afterwards it opens normally.
Then allow Bluetooth. macOS asks once, the first time KopiMeter looks for your device. If you miss the prompt, turn it on under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth — without it the app runs but never finds the device.
The app must stay running — it's what talks to Anthropic and feeds your device over Bluetooth. It starts by itself when you sign in; leave it there and forget it.

4✓

Connect your Claude account

KopiMeter never asks for your Claude password. It reads the sign-in Claude Code already stores on your computer — so you sign in there once, with Anthropic's own command. Open PowerShellTerminal and run:

claude login

A browser window opens — sign in with the same account you use in the Claude app, then close the terminal.

Once, ever. KopiMeter keeps the sign-in fresh by itself. You'd only repeat this if you log out of Claude or change your password.

5✓

Personalise it from your phone

Click the cup icon in your tray → Open setup page. Two green badges mean everything works: Claude Code login and Device. Scan the QR at the bottom of that page with your phone (same Wi-Fi) and it opens there, already signed in — nothing to type.

Set the fresh-pot chime (off by default) and the clock (enables the standby face and night dimming) if you want them.

Within seconds of the badges going green, the glass on your desk fills to your real level. That's setup done.

If something looks wrong

Troubleshooting

The app names its own problem — hover the tray icon for status, then open the match.

The device shows “No computer” or “No pour yet”

The device is on, but the app isn't sending anything.

No computer means it hasn't found your machine at all — check the pairing. No pour yet means it's connected but no reading has arrived. Either way, check the cup icon is in your tray; if it isn't, open KopiMeter from the Start menu or Applications folder.

The tray says Scanning and stays there

The app can't find your device.

Check it's powered and nearby. Still stuck? Remove it in Bluetooth settings and pair again.

The tray says "token expired — run claude login"

Your Claude sign-in was revoked — usually a log-out or password change.

Open a terminal, run claude login once, close it.

The screen is blank

No power, or the display is asleep.

Reseat the USB-C cable, then tap the screen or press the middle button.

History says “Nothing brewed yet”

Nothing's wrong — there isn't enough history yet.

Leave it running; the line fills in over the first hour.

Still stuck? Open an issue with what the tray icon says — that's usually enough to solve it in one reply.

Privacy

What leaves your computer

Your Claude login never does. It stays in the file Claude Code created on your machine. The app reads it to make its own check — never uploaded, never shown on the setup page, never logged.

Your prompts and conversations are never touched. KopiMeter has no access to them.

Once a minute, one tiny request goes to Anthropic and the reply's limit headers are read. It's as small as a request can be, but it does count as activity on your account.

Your usage numbers go to the device over Bluetooth and nowhere else. No KopiMeter account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Questions

Common questions

Do I have to use Claude Code? I only use the Claude app.

Only for the one-time sign-in in step 4. Your limits are shared across the Claude app, the web, and Claude Code, so chatting in the app moves the glass the same way.

Does it work if my computer is asleep or off?

No — the app fetches the numbers. While the computer sleeps the device keeps showing the last level it received, and catches up within a minute of waking.

Can I use it with more than one computer?

Pair it with whichever machine you work on. One at a time; to move it, pair with the new machine and install the app there.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

The device doesn't — it uses Bluetooth to your computer. Your computer needs internet, which it already has.

Why does my usage sometimes jump?

Limits are reported by Anthropic for your whole account — a long session in the browser shows up in the glass too.

How do I turn the chime on or off?

Tray icon → Open setup page → the fresh-pot chime toggle. It's off by default.
